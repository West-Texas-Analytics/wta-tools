What this shows

How “likely cause” is guessed

  • Blank owner and legal: at least one row has neither.
  • Catch-all shape: 20 or more rows on one footprint, which usually means a default polygon that unmapped accounts were pointed at.
  • Mineral interests, business personal property, mobile home, utility: a row's state code starts with G, L, M or J. These often share land on purpose.
  • Duplicate account: every row has the same owner and the same legal description.
  • Same legal, different owners: often valid, such as undivided interests or heirs with their own accounts.
  • Different legals on one shape: usually a split or a separate tract that was never drawn.

Owners and legals are compared after removing spaces and punctuation. These are guesses to sort the review, not findings.
